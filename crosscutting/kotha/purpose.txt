PURPOSE:
  Kotha (কথা) — Bangla voice & conversational layer across all 29 products. Grounded answers only; router, not a walled garden.

SHOULD CONTAIN:
  - intent/ — NLU/intent classification
  - router/ — intent -> product/view routing
  - answers/ — grounded answer engine + source/last-verified metadata
  - widget/ — embeddable component every product mounts
  - channels/ — SMS fallback, offline cache

SEE: ADR-0001 (docs/decisions/ADR-0001-Repository-Structure.md)
